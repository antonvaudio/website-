Anton Vasiliadis is a Scotland-based sound designer, technician, and multimedia artist working across immersive performance, experimental audio, and interactive installations. With a background in music and sound production, Anton brings technical precision to conceptually-driven artistic practice.

A graduate of the University of Edinburgh's MSc in Sound Design and holding a BA in Music Production & Engineering, Anton's work is grounded in technical precision and conceptual experimentation, spanning installation art, live performance, and sonic research.

In 2025, Anton was awarded an Explore grant from Immersive Arts UK, supporting his development in immersive and interactive technologies. His growing body of work explores how sound, touch, and real-time systems can create meaningful encounters with technology.

Get in touch about commissions, collaborations, sound design, or anything else.
