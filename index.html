Anton Vasiliadis is a Scotland-based sound designer, technician, and multimedia artist working across immersive performance, experimental audio, and interactive installations. With a background in music production and sound design, he works across audio, immersive performance, and creative code.

A graduate of the University of Edinburgh's MSc in Sound Design and holding a BA in Music Production & Engineering, Anton's work is grounded in technical precision and conceptual exploration.

In 2025, Anton was awarded an Explore grant from Immersive Arts UK, supporting his development in immersive and interactive technologies. His growing body of work explores how sound, touch, and presence shape our understanding of digital and physical space.
